Cashmere is graded finer than almost any other fibre — and the sector deserves the same precision.

Cashmere Futures brings together perspectives that don't usually sit in the same room: herding communities, sustainability researchers, industry buyers, and policymakers. Each sees a different part of the supply chain — and each is missing pieces the others hold.

This platform is a starting point for closing that gap: a shared, evolving picture of the cashmere sector, and a place to find people already working on it.
