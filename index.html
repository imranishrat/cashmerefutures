Why cashmere futures?

Cashmere connects fragile ecosystems, pastoral livelihoods, traditional knowledge, processing technologies, global manufacturing and luxury markets.

These elements cannot be understood in isolation. Cashmere Futures examines their connections through a Transformative Supply Chain Research perspective — asking not simply how the existing system can be improved, but how it can be transformed.
