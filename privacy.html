Privacy

How we handle your data.

A short, plain explanation of what happens when you use this site.

Contact form

When you submit the form on our Collaborate page, the information you provide (name, email, organisation, and message) is sent via Formspree, a third-party form-processing service, to our email inbox. We use it only to respond to you. We don't sell or share it with anyone else.

Analytics

We use a privacy-friendly analytics tool that counts page visits without using cookies or tracking individuals across other websites. We can see how many people visit which pages, but not who you are.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log standard technical information (like IP address) as part of serving the site. This is governed by GitHub's own privacy policy, not ours.

Questions

If you have questions about how your data is handled, email us at hello@cashmerefutures.com.
