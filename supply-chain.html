Supply Chain

From highland pasture to finished garment.

Eight stages carry cashmere from a goat's winter undercoat to a finished piece on a shelf — each one changing who's involved, what skill it demands, and how much of the final value actually reaches the people at the start of the chain. This isn't just a description of how things are: each stage is also a point where the chain could genuinely be rebuilt, not just made greener.
