From fibre to garment

How cashmere moves through the supply chain

Cashmere is transformed through a sequence of biological, artisanal, industrial and commercial activities. The pathway is geographically dispersed and can vary by fibre quality, processing capability, ownership and market.

Value creation

Value is added unevenly

Economic value does not accumulate uniformly across the chain. Fibre characteristics, processing technology, market access, design, branding, certification and trading relationships can influence who captures value.

Transformation

Traceability matters

Each transition can create opportunities for better documentation, transparency and responsible production—from origin and primary processing to manufacturing and final markets.

Analytical note: This is a conceptual representation, not a single universal route. Cashmere supply chains can include additional trading, storage, transport, testing, certification and finishing activities, and stages may occur in different countries or overlap in practice.
