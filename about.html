About

Connecting the people, places and systems behind cashmere.

Cashmere Futures is a research and collaboration platform exploring how the cashmere system can become more sustainable, inclusive, transparent and resilient.

Cashmere is a fibre. Its future is a system question.

Herders know the land and livelihoods behind the fibre. Spinners and weavers carry technical and cultural knowledge. Researchers study environmental, social and market pressures. Industry shapes demand, sourcing and innovation. Policymakers and standards bodies influence the rules under which the sector operates.

Cashmere Futures brings these perspectives into one evolving platform — connecting evidence, people and places so that the opportunities and challenges across the supply chain can be understood together.

How we work

01 — 05
01

Community-first

Communities are primary stakeholders whose knowledge, priorities and lived experience should shape research and transformation.

02

Evidence over assumption

Verifiable evidence and transparent sources matter — especially where sustainability and provenance claims affect livelihoods and markets.

03

Transformation, not just protection

The goal is not simply to make the existing system less harmful, but to understand what structural changes could improve outcomes.

04

Connected thinking

Environmental, social, technological, economic and governance issues are treated as connected parts of the same supply system.

05

Open collaboration

Cashmere Futures is intended to connect and amplify existing knowledge rather than duplicate or replace the work already being done.

A platform for research, evidence and dialogue.

Cashmere Futures is an emerging initiative, and some parts of the platform will continue to develop. The ambition, however, is clear: to build a useful and credible space for understanding the cashmere system and exploring pathways toward its transformation.

Help shape the future of cashmere.

Researchers, communities, industry, policymakers and organisations can all contribute knowledge to this evolving picture.
