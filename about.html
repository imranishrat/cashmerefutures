About Cashmere Futures

A research platform for transformative cashmere supply chains.

Cashmere Futures brings together research, community knowledge, industry experience and policy perspectives to understand how the cashmere supply system can change.

Cashmere is a fibre. Its future is a system question.

Cashmere connects pastoral landscapes, livelihoods, traditional knowledge, processing technologies, manufacturing, luxury markets and institutions.

Cashmere Futures examines those connections through a Transformative Supply Chain Research perspective — with a particular interest in the people and places often least visible in global luxury supply chains.

Why transformative research?

Conventional supply-chain improvement often focuses on efficiency, risk reduction or incremental sustainability. A transformative perspective asks a broader question: what needs to change in the system itself?

For cashmere, that means considering relationships among people, planet, technology and governance — and identifying pathways toward systemic transformation.
