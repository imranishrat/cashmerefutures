Trade-data qualification. HS classification matters. HS 510531 covers fine animal hair of Kashmir (cashmere) goats, carded or combed; HS 510211 covers the same animal hair not carded or combed. These are distinct trade classifications and should not be combined without a defined conversion methodology. Trade values and quantities describe cross-border transactions, not production. Where a source reports an aggregate cashmere-products value, it is retained as an aggregate trade indicator rather than presented as a fibre value.

Evidence standard. The Library will prioritise customs statistics and UN Comtrade-derived observations with an identifiable HS code, reporting country, year and flow. Shipment-level commercial databases may be used only as corroborating evidence and will not be treated as national totals. Missing quantities remain missing rather than being reconstructed from price assumptions.
