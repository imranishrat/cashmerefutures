Communities

Cashmere communities at the centre.

Exploring livelihoods, wellbeing, inclusion, resilience and opportunities for the communities whose lives are connected to cashmere — from herding households to artisans and cooperatives.

The people behind the fibre rarely appear in the conversation about it.

[Draft — please edit] Cashmere begins with herding households — often in remote, high-altitude, or arid regions — whose livelihoods depend on animals that are increasingly exposed to climate pressure, market volatility, and land change. Their knowledge and priorities are essential to any serious conversation about the sector's future, yet they're frequently the last to be consulted about it.

This section is where we bring their perspective forward — not as a footnote to industry or research, but as the starting point.
