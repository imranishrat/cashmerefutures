Research

Research for transformative cashmere futures.

Cashmere Futures investigates how the cashmere system can be transformed — socially, environmentally, technologically and economically — through evidence, interdisciplinary research and collaboration.

From a finer fibre to a better system.

Cashmere is not only a fibre. It is a connected system of landscapes, livelihoods, processing technologies, markets, institutions and cultural knowledge.

Our research focuses on the points where that system can change: where value is created and distributed, where environmental and social pressures emerge, and where new technologies, governance arrangements and forms of collaboration can support more resilient and equitable futures.

Understanding how technology, process innovation and new forms of collaboration can reshape the future of luxury apparel supply chains.

The Cashmere Futures Framework.

We view transformation through five connected lenses: people, planet, technology, governance and value. These lenses help connect community realities with supply-chain evidence and practical opportunities for change.

Research on this platform is intended to be useful beyond academia — for communities, practitioners, industry, policymakers and other researchers working to understand and improve the cashmere system.
