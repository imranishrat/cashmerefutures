Transformative Supply Chain Research

Research for transformative cashmere futures.

Cashmere Futures investigates how the cashmere supply system can be transformed through evidence-based research on people, planet, technology and governance.

From improving the chain to transforming the system.

Cashmere connects fragile ecosystems, pastoral livelihoods, traditional knowledge, processing technologies, global manufacturing and luxury markets.

Transformative Supply Chain Research asks not simply how the existing system can become more efficient or sustainable, but how relationships, structures and practices across the system can change to produce better environmental and social outcomes.

Research that connects evidence to transformation.

Methods may include qualitative inquiry, case studies, supply-chain analysis, secondary research, multi-criteria decision-making and AI-enabled approaches. The method follows the research question rather than the other way around.

The programme is designed to connect academic research with practical knowledge held by communities, practitioners, industry and policymakers.
